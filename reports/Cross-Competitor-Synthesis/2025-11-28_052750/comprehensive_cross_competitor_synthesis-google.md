*   **Capitalize on Competitor Stumbles:** CAE is facing financial headwinds and contract losses; Elbit is operationally constrained by the Israel-Hamas war, delaying exports. Red 6 can market itself as the reliable, US-centric alternative for allied nations (UK, NATO) frustrated by these supply chain shocks.
