### 2. IAF F‑16 MTC & PLAF F‑16 MTC

- **Relevance to Red 6:**

  - Willingness to spend 9‑figure sums on high‑fidelity sim plus multi‑decade O&M.

- **Threat to Red 6:**  
  - Elbit can bundle any future AR‑enabled cockpit solutions or AR pilot aids as “extensions” of these MTCs, offering a fully integrated training continuum (sim‑to‑live).  
  - Red 6 will need to position its AR as:
    - Either a lower‑cost, higher‑fidelity live complement to existing Elbit sims, or

### 3. IDF MBT Simulation & Asia‑Pac EW Training

- **Relevance:**

### 4. UK Training Megaprogram (Pipeline)

- **If Elbit loses:**
