## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Strategic Situation Overview

Red 6 occupies a uniquely differentiated position in the military training market with its ATARS (Airborne Tactical Augmented Reality System) technology. Red 6 is positioned as the first company in the world delivering real-time, in-flight synthetic air combat training directly into the cockpits of operational fighter jets. This is a genuine first-mover advantage that competitors cannot easily replicate.

### Key Recent Milestones
- Red 6 has been awarded a contract from the U.S. Air Force to integrate ATARS into the F-16 Fighting Falcon, building on successful integration into the T-38 Talon.
- The system will be initially integrated into two F-16s over the next 12 to 18 months.
- Red 6 has integrated its ATARS technology with Boeing's AH-64E Apache Crewstation Advanced Technology Testbed (CATT), marking expansion into rotary-wing platforms.
- In 2024, Red 6 completed ATARS demonstration flights with the UK Royal Air Force to assess the viability of augmented reality aboard the service's BAE Systems Hawk T2 trainers.

### Critical Strategic Findings

**1. Competitive Moat is Strong but Time-Limited**
Red 6's real-time outdoor AR capability remains unmatched. "ATARS is the only system capable of replicating the cognitive complexity fighter pilots face in real-world engagements." However, major defense primes are investing heavily in adjacent technologies.

**2. Partnership Ecosystem is Robust**
- Red 6 is an LM Ventures portfolio company, and since announcing their partnership, Red 6 and Lockheed Martin continue close collaboration on advanced mission training across live, virtual and constructive environments.
- Boeing and Red 6 have integrated and flown an augmented reality pilot training system in a TA-4J tactical aircraft, with Boeing being the first company to team with Red 6 on this type of advanced training technology.

**3. Market Timing is Favorable**
Red 6's ATARS platform creates virtual outdoor worlds that deliver threat-relevant, near-peer level training scenarios at speed and scale—affordably increasing safety, easing airspace limitations, enabling next-generation collaborative combat aircraft (CCA) training and providing significant environmental benefits.

### Red 6's Unique Differentiator

Using a specially designed wide field-of-view AR headset that integrates with standard flight helmets, ATARS projects synthetic elements—enemy aircraft, missiles, refueling tankers, or friendly assets—directly into the pilot's view of the real world. Unlike simulator-based training, ATARS allows crews to experience the physiological and cognitive demands of actual flight—G forces, atmospheric conditions, and workload—while interacting with virtual threats and assets.

### Competitor-Specific Capabilities

**Elbit Systems:**
- The IDF's new MBT training systems utilize Elbit's cloud-native platform-agnostic OneSim™ simulation software infrastructure and include high fidelity platform trainer turrets with accurate physical models.
- With the completion of final trials in late 2024, Elbit delivered its sixth and final JFST training platform type. As of January 2025, JFST accumulated 12,000 hours of training with Army soldiers.

**Thales:**
- When designing the GLADIATOR live tactical engagement training system, Thales had three key goals: achieve the optimal balance between complexity and fidelity, offer a versatile solution capable of simulating the widest possible range of situations, and maximum interoperability.
- Thales remains at the forefront of the next generation of military synthetic training and has developed training technology and services to meet the training needs of over 30 military services, including the U.S. Air Force.

**CAE:**
- CAE's Defense & Security business unit is a globally recognized training and mission systems integrator, at the leading edge of digital innovation providing training and mission support solutions across multi-domain operations – air, land, maritime, space and cyber.
