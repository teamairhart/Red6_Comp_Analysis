# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## Critical Strategic Development: IVAS/SBMC Program Takeover

Anduril Industries has achieved a transformational competitive position in the HMD/AR/military training domain through a series of strategic contract wins and program acquisitions over the past 24 months. The most significant development is Anduril's takeover of the U.S. Army's IVAS (Integrated Visual Augmentation System) program from Microsoft, which represents **the single largest competitive threat to Red 6's market position**.

### Key Findings:

1. **IVAS Contract Novation (February-April 2025)**: On April 10, the service signed off on the "contact novation," which essentially slid the terms of Microsoft's 10-year, $22 billion contract over to the tech company, according to Anduril's Senior Vice President of Engineering Tom Keane.

2. **SBMC Prototype Award (September 2025)**: Anduril Industries announced it has been awarded a $159 million contract by the U.S. Army for an initial prototyping period to develop a night vision and mixed reality system as part of the Soldier Borne Mission Command program.

3. **Market Position**: The company reported a doubling of annual revenue in 2024, reaching approximately $1 billion. The IVAS takeover could potentially triple this revenue.

### Program History & Transfer:

In 2018, the Army picked Microsoft's commercially available HoloLens 2 heads-up display for the IVAS program and eventually awarded the company a 10-year, $22 billion production deal.

Microsoft won a 10-year contract worth nearly $22 billion to build more than 120,000 custom HoloLens headsets for the Army in 2021, but the company discontinued production of the device last year.

Microsoft is seeking to transfer its entire IVAS contract over to Anduril. The move — which would see Anduril assume oversight of the entire program, including development of the current hardware and software, hiring decisions on staff and takeover of production — comes as the Army is considering launching a follow-on IVAS competition.

### Pre-Transfer Integration Work:

In September 2024, the tech startup announced its steps toward assuming the IVAS contract with a Microsoft partnership for the integration of new sensors and the Lattice platform into the device.

### RED 6 COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS - CRITICAL:

This represents the most significant competitive threat to Red 6. In February, the U.S. Army reassigned a high-profile augmented and virtual reality headset contract—initially awarded to Microsoft—to Anduril. The contract, tied to a $22 billion budget, is viewed as a strategic win that places Anduril at the center of next-generation military interface development.

**Threat Rating: SEVERE** - Anduril is now positioned as the Army's primary partner for soldier-worn AR/MR systems, directly competing with Red 6's airborne training focus.

### Contract Details:

The U.S. Army has awarded contracts with a combined total of $354 million to Anduril Industries and Rivet Industries to develop and deliver new warfighter capabilities as part of the Soldier Borne Mission Command, or SBMC, program. Anduril received a $159 million contract, while Rivet executed a $195 million deal for the SBMC.

Formerly known as the next-generation Integrated Visual Augmented System, or IVAS, the SBMC is intended to create a day/night situational awareness and mission command platform for the Army. The technology envisioned must have backwards compatibility with the Android Tactical Assault Kit, or ATAK, according to a request for solutions posted on SAM.gov in April.

### Technical Scope:

The contract covers an initial prototyping phase for what the service describes as a next-generation interface combining night vision, augmented reality, and integrated command-and-control tools. The goal is to provide soldiers with real-time access to tactical information while reducing reliance on separate radios, maps, and apps in contested environments.

### Team Structure:

Anduril is working with Meta, OSI, Qualcomm, and Gentex to build the hardware. The helmet-mounted display will integrate day, night, and thermal imagery with real-time intelligence.

Built on Anduril's Lattice platform, SBMC-A is led by Anduril in partnership with Palantir Technologies, L3Harris Technologies; Persistent Systems, LLC; Sierra Nevada Company; DTC; Maxar Intelligence; Kägwerks; and others.

### Competitive Landscape:

Rivet Industries, a defense tech startup founded in 2024 by a group of former Palantir executives, was awarded $195 million to test, iterate, develop, and help field whatever headset turns out to work best for Army soldiers.

Rivet CEO David Marra, who previously headed up the Microsoft team that held the original IVAS contract, stated "We have an incredibly legitimate solution that exists today, and one that we've tested in the field numerous times, including with Army soldiers in operational conditions."

### RED 6 COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS:

**Threat Rating: HIGH** - While focused on ground forces rather than aviation, Anduril's SBMC solution demonstrates their expanding HMD/AR capabilities. Key concern areas:
- Technology spillover potential to aviation applications
- Anduril-Meta partnership accessing consumer AR technology for military use
- Establishing Anduril as DoD's preferred AR/MR partner

## 1.4 OTHER SIGNIFICANT ANDURIL CONTRACTS (Non-HMD/Training)

While not directly in the HMD/AR/training space, these contracts demonstrate Anduril's growing DoD relationship and financial capacity:

### Counter-UAS Contracts:

Anduril Industries has won a potential 10-year, $642.2 million contract from the U.S. Navy to install, deliver and sustain Installation-Counter small Unmanned Aircraft Systems, or IC-sUAS, at Marine Corps bases worldwide.

The Department of Defense said Marine Corps Systems Command launched a competitive procurement process and received 10 offers for the indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract. The majority of contract work will occur in Costa Mesa, California, and the rest will be performed in Washington, D.C., and at various Marine Corps installations through March 2035.

The U.S. Navy has awarded two contracts worth $400 million combined to Invariant and Anduril Industries to integrate and deliver a counter-unmanned aircraft system engagement system, or CES, and provide related hardware, software and support services for the military branch. Each firm-fixed-price IDIQ contract is valued at $200 million.

### Air Defense:

The Department of Defense has awarded defense technology company Anduril Industries a $250 million contract to strengthen air defense capabilities across all military services. Anduril said the contract covers the delivery of more than 500 Roadrunner-Ms and additional Pulsar electronic warfare capabilities.

### AFWERX/Thunderdome:

Anduril received a $99 million Thunderdome contract from the Air Force. AFWERX, the Air Force's innovation arm based in Dayton, Ohio, serves as the contracting activity. It will allocate an initial amount of $8.4 million from fiscal 2024 research, development, test and evaluation funds.

# PART 3: TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT & CAPABILITIES ANALYSIS

## 3.1 Anduril HMD Technical Capabilities

### Hardware Architecture:

Under the Army award, Anduril will build an initial prototype of its proposed night vision and mixed reality system for the SBMC. The helmet-mounted technology combines day, night and thermal imagery with real-time battlefield intelligence.

Anduril's solution reimagines the battlefield interface giving soldiers superhero-like abilities. In collaboration with leading technology partners—including Meta; OSI; Qualcomm Technologies, Inc.; and Gentex Corporation—Anduril is developing a helmet-mounted mixed reality system that unites advanced night vision with augmented reality overlays. This creates a single perceptual layer that fuses day, night, and thermal imagery with real-time battlefield intelligence.

### Software Platform (SBMC-A / Lattice):

Software update timelines have been reduced by 99 per cent, from two days to just 15 minutes, enabling daily updates in the field and faster delivery of new capabilities.

The system has undergone four soldier exercises and combat training scenarios, including demonstrations of drone command and control directly from the headset without a dedicated pilot. 14 industry partners are engaged in SBMC-A, with third-party developers already onboarded via the Lattice Software Development Kit.

### Field Testing:

Anduril reports successful demonstrations where operators used Lattice-integrated headsets to task unmanned aerial systems at distances beyond three kilometres via line-of-sight radio links, without a dedicated drone pilot.

Anduril said it has drawn on over 260,000 hours of soldier input from the IVAS programme and has already integrated IVAS 1.2 headsets with Lattice for field testing.

# PART 4: RED 6 CONTRACT ACTIVITY COMPARISON

## 4.1 Recent Red 6 Awards

### F-16 ATARS Integration Contract (August 2025):

Red 6, a hardware-enabled software company revolutionizing military flight training, announced it has been awarded a contract from the U.S. Air Force to integrate its ATARS (Airborne Tactical Augmented Reality System) into the F-16 Fighting Falcon. Awarded through Air Combat Command and the Air Force Research Laboratory, this milestone positions Red 6 as the first company in the world delivering real-time, in-flight synthetic air combat training directly into the cockpits of operational fighter jets.

It will be initially integrated into two F-16s over the next 12 to 18 months, Red 6 CEO and former British Royal Air Force pilot Daniel Robinson informed Breaking Defense.

### Historical Context:

The U.S. Air Force has already contracted Red 6 in 2021 under a deal worth up to $70 million to integrate ATARS on the T-38 Talon, with further testing on the MC-130 as part of a contract awarded in 2024.

The 5-year development contract with the USAF, issued in 2021, continues, with another $10m of funding recently added; Red 6 has also won a Strategic Funding Increase (STRATFI) award from the USAF.

### Platform Coverage:

In addition to the F-16 and the T-38, ATARS is already integrated into the MC-130 with the U.S. Air Force and the BAE Systems Hawk T-2 with the U.K. Royal Air Force. Red 6 also holds active integration partnerships with Boeing, Aeralis, Palantir, SNC, and Northrop Grumman.

### Apache Helicopter Integration:

The integration not only marks the first time an AR flight training system of this kind has been integrated with a rotorcraft platform, but also expands the company's reach beyond its established Air Force partnerships to include U.S. Army platforms.

## 5.2 Market Segment Overlap Assessment

### Areas of Direct Competition:

1. **Army Rotorcraft Training**: "Exploring the integration of ATARS with the Apache marks a significant milestone – not only as our inaugural collaboration with a rotorcraft platform but also as our first potential partnership with a U.S. Army platform," said Daniel Robinson. This creates direct competition with Anduril's Army-focused HMD solutions.

2. **Technology Investment**: Anduril's access to Meta's consumer AR R&D investment creates an asymmetric competitive advantage in hardware development.

3. **Software Platform Ecosystem**: Anduril's Lattice SDK approach vs. Red 6's CARBON distributed training environment represent competing architecture philosophies.

### Areas of Market Differentiation:

1. **Aviation-Specific Focus**: Red 6 maintains advantage in airborne-specific AR training (dynamic outdoor environments, high-speed maneuvering, g-forces).

2. **Live Flight Training**: Unlike simulator-based training, ATARS allows crews to experience the physiological and cognitive demands of actual flight—G forces, atmospheric conditions, and workload—while interacting with virtual threats and assets.

3. **Air Force Relationships**: Red 6's established relationships with ACC, AETC, and AFSOC provide competitive moat in Air Force markets.

## 5.3 Future Pipeline Threats

### Anduril Position on Follow-on Programs:

By taking over management of the current contract just ahead of findings of a make-or-break operational assessment, Luckey's team would have additional time working with Army leaders on the program, and access to a mix of production and research and development funding. One industry source said it's that access to government funding that will give Anduril an advantage over other IVAS Next competitors.

### Modular Platform Strategy:

Palmer Luckey stated: "Like, the guy who flies an Osprey [tiltrotor aircraft] probably should have a different system than a guy who is driving a tank. It's just, not a little different — it's a lot different." He noted that this move away from one device for all users marks one of the most significant differences between Anduril's and Microsoft's approaches.

This modular approach could eventually position Anduril to compete for aviation-specific HMD contracts.

# PART 6: STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS FOR RED 6

## 6.1 Immediate Actions

1. **Accelerate Army Platform Integration**: The Apache integration should be prioritized to establish Red 6 presence in Army aviation before Anduril's SBMC technology matures.

2. **Protect Air Force Position**: Deepen relationships with ACC, AETC, and AFSOC through expanded platform coverage and enhanced CARBON capabilities.

3. **International Market Expansion**: Leverage UK RAF relationship (Hawk T-2) to expand NATO and Five Eyes presence before Anduril's international footprint grows.

## 6.2 Technology Investment Priorities

1. **Outdoor AR Display Performance**: Maintain technology leadership in high-brightness, high-resolution outdoor AR displays - a capability gap Anduril must still address.

2. **Multi-ship Networked Operations**: Continue development of CARBON distributed training environment as differentiated capability.

3. **Integration Speed**: Demonstrate ability to integrate across platforms faster than Anduril's ground-focused approach.

## 6.3 Competitive Positioning

- Position Red 6 as the **aviation training specialist** vs. Anduril's **ground force situational awareness** focus
- Emphasize live flight training advantages that ground-focused HMDs cannot replicate
- Build coalition with aviation OEMs (Boeing, Lockheed Martin) that may be cautious about Anduril's expanding footprint
