## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

BAE Systems represents a significant player in the HMD and military training technology space, with active investments in both aviation helmet-mounted displays and synthetic training environments. The company maintains a **partnership with Red 6** rather than pursuing a directly competitive approach, though their capabilities could evolve to become competitive over time.

### Key Investment Priorities (2023-2025):
1. **Striker II HMD Development** - Major £173M+ investment for Eurofighter Typhoon integration
2. **Project OdySSEy** - Single synthetic environment for multi-domain training
3. **Red 6 Partnership** - Integration of ATARS into Hawk trainer and OdySSEy platform

### Strategic Assessment:
BAE Systems has entered into an agreement with Red 6 to integrate advanced tactical augmented reality systems into Hawk trainer aircraft. This partnership positions BAE as a potential channel partner rather than a direct competitor, though the relationship requires monitoring for strategic shifts.

#### System Description:
Project OdySSEy is a highly realistic, cloud-based, single synthetic environment that represents the next generation of military training. It is a platform with multiple embedded technologies, creating a 'digital realm' for training purposes and beyond. It brings together expertise in simulation, supercomputing, data analytics, augmented and virtual reality and artificial intelligence to provide unpredictable scenarios to the users.

#### Partnership Ecosystem:
Project OdySSEy is enabled by partnerships with a number of leading technology providers and training experts. From BAE Systems' role as a Lead Systems Integrator (LSI) they collaborate with data analytics provider VRAI, simulation specialists Bohemia Interactive Simulations, Pitch Technologies, PLEXSYS, VR/AR experts Pauley Interactive, Hadean – the supercomputing specialist with the Guinness world record for most concurrent players in an online game, military training specialists D3A Defence and Inzpire.

#### AI/ML Integration:
BAE Systems and Hadean joined forces to exploit state-of-the-art AI and Large Language Model (LLM) integrations of the Hadean Platform and power the next generation of synthetic training environments.

The capability focuses on developing a largescale synthetic training environment designed to support comprehensive multi-domain military exercises. It integrates high-fidelity artificial intelligence to simulate complex patterns of life, creating a realistic and immersive training platform for forces operating in air, land, sea, space, and cyber domains.

## PART 2: RED 6 PARTNERSHIP ANALYSIS

### 2.1 Partnership Structure

BAE Systems has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Red 6, an augmented reality technology firm at the forefront of synthetic air combat training. The collaboration will explore ways to combine BAE Systems' experience in delivering training to military forces alongside its expertise in helmet-mounted displays with Red 6's breakthrough ATARS technology.

**Announcement Venue:** I/ITSEC 2022, Orlando, Florida (November 29, 2022)

### 2.2 Integration into Project OdySSEy

BAE Systems has revealed plans to integrate Red 6's Advanced Tactical Augmented Reality Systems (ATARS) and PLEXSYS' Advanced Simulation Combat Operations Trainer into its Project OdySSEy single synthetic environment.

Red 6 is working with BAE Systems and US software modelling and simulation systems specialist Plexsys Interface Products on BAE Systems' Project Odyssey. This project aims to build a common synthetic military training environment that will allow fighters using it to train together inside a single AR battlespace.

### 2.3 Hawk Integration Potential

Using Red 6's Advanced Tactical Augmented Reality Systems (ATARS), Hawk pilots will be able to see, identify and engage virtual threats and cooperate with virtual wingmen while airborne.

Unlike some other AR technologies, ATARS can work outdoors in a high-speed and dynamic environment.

### 2.4 BAE's Training Heritage

BAE Systems has been a leader in the fast jet augmented reality market for over 60 years. The Company developed the world's first heads up display (HUD) for the Buccaneer in 1958 and the family of Striker Helmet Mounted Display Systems (HMDS) used on Typhoon and Gripen aircraft.

BAE Systems is global leader in this field and today it trains more than 6,000 people every year for military forces across the world, including hundreds of aircrew, technicians and specialists roles.

## PART 6: RED 6 COMPETITIVE IMPLICATIONS

### 6.1 Direct Threat Assessment: **LOW**

BAE Systems has explicitly chosen partnership over competition with Red 6. Working with Red 6 takes this capability a step further for the pilots of tomorrow, by bringing constructed adversaries, wingmen and surface threats into the training space at a fraction of the cost and emissions of the live equivalent.

### 6.2 Partnership Opportunity: **HIGH**

Current partnership elements present expansion opportunities:
- **Hawk Integration:** Global fleet of 650+ aircraft represents significant market
- **OdySSEy Integration:** ATARS as the airborne AR component of multi-domain SSE
- **Striker II Potential:** Future integration of ATARS symbology into Striker II HMD hardware

### 6.3 Strategic Risks to Monitor:

1. **Capability Internalization:** BAE could develop competing in-flight AR capability if partnership terms unfavorable
2. **Alternative AR Partners:** BAE could partner with ATARS competitors (e.g., ASELSAN TULGAR)
3. **Vertical Integration:** BAE acquisition of AR startup to compete with ATARS

### 6.4 First-Mover Advantages to Protect:

- **Outdoor AR Performance:** BAE explicitly acknowledged ATARS' unique outdoor capability
- **Multi-Aircraft Networking:** Continue advancing networked training capabilities
- **Platform Breadth:** Expand beyond current platforms before BAE seeks alternatives

### 6.5 Technology Gaps to Address:

1. **LLM/AI Integration:** BAE and Hadean are exploiting LLM integrations - Red 6 should ensure competitive AI capabilities
2. **Multi-Domain Integration:** Project OdySSEy spans air, land, sea, space, cyber - ATARS currently air-focused
3. **Data Analytics:** BAE partnering with VRAI for training analytics - Red 6 should enhance data/insights capabilities
