Analyze the technology evolution and capability trends in helmet-mounted displays, augmented reality, simulation, and military training systems for [COMPANY NAME] with respect to fixed-wing aviation / rotary aviation / ground forces.

RESEARCH REQUIREMENTS:

1. TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE:
- Search patents filed by the company and any of its subsidiaries in last 5 years
- Keywords: "helmet mounted display", "head-up display", "augmented reality", "see-through display", "visor projection", "simulation", "synthetic training", "LVC", "live virtual constructive", "military training"
- Identify: optical architectures (waveguide vs. combiner vs. direct retinal), tracking methods (magnetic vs. optical vs. inertial), resolution specifications, field-of-view angles

2. CAPABILITY TRENDS:
- What new capabilities are being added? (e.g., AR overlays, sensor fusion, AI-enhanced symbology, night vision integration)
- Search for: conference proceedings (I/ITSEC, Interservice/Industry Training, SPIE Defense + Commercial Sensing), white papers, military requirements documents
- Identify specific pilot/operator pain points being addressed

3. TECHNOLOGY READINESS:
- Assess maturity: which capabilities are fielded vs. in testing vs. conceptual
- Search for: military test reports, operational assessments, GAO reports, DOTE (Director of Operational Test & Evaluation) publications

4. SUPPLIER ECOSYSTEM:
- Identify component suppliers (display engines, optics, tracking sensors)
- Map dependencies: which companies rely on the same suppliers?
- Search LinkedIn for supply chain relationships, partnership announcements

5. TECHNICAL BARRIERS:
- What challenges are repeatedly mentioned? (latency, weight, power consumption, thermal management, sunlight readability)
- Search for: academic papers, program cancellation notices, solicitation requirements that mention "overcoming limitations"

DEFENSE-SPECIFIC SOURCES:
- Check DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center) for technical reports
- Search Congressional Research Service reports for program background
- Review DoD budget justification books (PB-44s) for program funding
- Check DOTE annual reports for operational test results
