Research government procurement activity and contract awards for [COMPANY NAME] and all subsidiaries relevant to helmet-mounted displays, augmented reality, military training, and related emerging technologies.

GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE: Primarily US, but also international arms and subsidiaries.

This reveals market traction and future opportunities.

DATA SOURCES TO SEARCH:
- USASpending.gov (US federal contracts)
- SAM.gov (active solicitations)
- FedBizOpps/beta.SAM.gov archives (historical RFPs)
- Defense Contract Management Agency notices
- Foreign Military Sales (FMS) notices
- International defense procurement sites (UK, Australia, NATO nations)

ANALYSIS STRUCTURE:

1. HISTORICAL CONTRACT AWARDS (5-year lookback):
For each company, extract:
- Contract number, award date, awarding agency/service branch
- Contract value (obligated vs. potential total)
- Program name and platform (if specified)
- Contract type (FFP, CPFF, IDIQ, OTA)
- Period of performance
- Contract modifications (shows program health/growth)

2. ACTIVE PROGRAMS:
- Which programs are currently in production/delivery?
- Production quantities (if disclosed)
- Delivery schedules
- Performance metrics or milestones mentioned

3. UPCOMING OPPORTUNITIES:
- Search for open solicitations or RFIs related to HMD
- Keywords: "helmet mounted", "head-worn display", "pilot display", "crew station", "augmented reality", "military training", "flight training", "LVC", "live virtual constructive", "synthetic training", "simulation", specific platform modernization programs
- Note: requirement release date, proposal due date, estimated award date
- Extract key requirements that indicate customer priorities

4. PROGRAM HEALTH INDICATORS:
- Search for GAO reports, Inspector General audits, DOTE assessments
- Look for program delays, cost overruns, performance issues
- Identify Nunn-McCurdy breaches or program restructures
- Check Congressional testimony or hearing records

5. INTERNATIONAL SALES:
- FMS notifications for equipment or training systems
- Which countries are buying from which vendors?
- Are there regional preferences (European buyers prefer European vendors)?

6. COMPETITIVE WIN/LOSS PATTERNS:
- Which companies win which types of competitions?
- Are there platform-specific incumbents?
- Down-select patterns (who makes it to final competitive rounds?)

7. PRICING INTELLIGENCE:
- Where disclosed, extract unit pricing
- Calculate price per unit trends over time
- Compare pricing across similar systems (indicates market positioning)

DEFENSE-SPECIFIC SOURCES:
- Check DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center) for technical reports
- Search Congressional Research Service reports for program background
- Review DoD budget justification books (PB-44s) for program funding
- Check DOTE annual reports for operational test results

SOURCE HIERARCHY (prioritize in this order):
TIER 1 (Highest reliability):
- Government contract databases (USASpending.gov, SAM.gov)
- Official DoD announcements
- GAO and Inspector General reports
- Congressional Research Service reports

TIER 2 (Good reliability):
- Trade publications (Defense News, Breaking Defense, Janes)
- Company annual reports / 10-Ks and earnings calls
- FMS notifications

TIER 3 (Use with caution - mark as [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]):
- News aggregators
- Blog posts
- Company press releases (may be optimistic)

CRITICAL VERIFICATION STEPS:
- All contract numbers must be verified in official databases
- Cross-check award values with multiple sources (initial release vs. USASpending.gov vs. company earnings calls)
- Distinguish between contract ceiling value and actual obligations
- Note if information comes from company press release vs. government source (company claims may be optimistic)
- Flag any information from single source as [SINGLE SOURCE - VERIFY]

HALLUCINATION PREVENTION:
- Do not infer or extrapolate beyond what sources explicitly state
- If you cannot find information, state "No data found" rather than estimating
- Mark any uncertainty with [CONFIDENCE: LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH]
- Do not estimate contract values - provide only verified numbers
- Do not assume contract awards without verifying in official databases
- Provide exact contract numbers for all claims

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Well-organized markdown file with:
- Table with all contracts (contract number, value, date, agency, program)
- Summary of total contract value by company (last 5 years)
- Active programs list with status
- Pipeline analysis of upcoming opportunities
- Market share estimates based on obligated contract values
- Confidence Assessment rating each major finding as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW
