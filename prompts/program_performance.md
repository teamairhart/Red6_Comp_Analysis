You are conducting program execution and contract performance intelligence analysis for defense technology competitive intelligence.

RESEARCH TARGET: [COMPANY NAME] and all subsidiaries with active contracts in helmet-mounted displays, augmented reality, military training, and simulation.

TIME FRAME: Last 36 months (to capture program lifecycle data).

PART 1 - ACTIVE PROGRAM STATUS:
For each major program in the HMD/AR/training portfolio:

Required data points:
- Program name and customer
- Contract value and period of performance
- Current program phase (development, LRIP, FRP, sustainment)
- Schedule status: On track? Delayed? Ahead of schedule?
- Cost status: Within budget? Cost growth? If overrun, by what percentage?
- Technical performance: Meeting specifications? Waivers or deviations granted?

Search sources (in priority order):
1. DOT&E (Director, Operational Test & Evaluation) Annual Reports
2. GAO reports on specific programs
3. Congressional testimony and hearing transcripts
4. Service acquisition status reports
5. Trade publication investigative reporting

PART 2 - SCHEDULE AND COST PERFORMANCE:
Specific metrics to investigate:
- Milestone achievements vs. original schedule
- Engineering change proposals (indicate scope creep or technical issues)
- Contract modifications (cost growth, schedule extensions)
- Nunn-McCurdy breaches (for major defense programs)
- Production rate achieved vs. planned

Search: SAM.gov for contract modifications, GAO cost/schedule assessments, CRS reports

PART 3 - TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE AND TESTING:
- Operational test results (pass/fail, with conditions)
- User feedback and soldier/pilot acceptance
- Known technical issues or deficiencies
- Reliability and maintainability data
- Safety issues or incidents

Search: DOT&E reports, service test agency reports, Congressional testimony, trade publication coverage

PART 4 - CUSTOMER SATISFACTION INDICATORS:
- Follow-on contract awards (indicates satisfaction)
- Contract terminations or reductions
- Customer complaints in public record (GAO, Congressional hearings)
- International customer repeat business
- Loss of incumbent position to competitor

PART 5 - CONTRACTOR PAST PERFORMANCE:
- CPARS (Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System) ratings if accessible via FOIA
- Past performance cited in competitive evaluations (from GAO protest decisions)
- Industry reputation assessments from trade publications

PART 6 - PRODUCTION AND DELIVERY PERFORMANCE:
- Units delivered vs. contracted quantities
- Delivery schedule adherence
- Quality escapes or field returns
- Supply chain issues affecting delivery

PART 7 - PROGRAM RISK INDICATORS:
Red flags to investigate:
- Program manager changes (multiple changes indicate trouble)
- Major subcontractor changes
- Organizational conflicts of interest issues
- Cybersecurity incidents
- Workforce issues (strikes, layoffs affecting program)

Search: Defense trade publications, Congressional testimony, company announcements

PART 8 - COMPARATIVE PROGRAM PERFORMANCE:
How does [COMPANY]'s program performance compare to:
- Industry averages for similar programs
- Competitor performance on comparable programs
- Their own historical performance trends
