Map the partnership ecosystem for development and delivery of helmet-mounted display, augmented reality or military training systems for [COMPANY NAME] and subsidiaries.

TIME FRAME: Last 5 years (partnerships formed, modified, or active).

NOTE: This prompt focuses exclusively on the competitor ecosystem, not partnerships for Red 6.

INVESTIGATION AREAS:

1. PRIME/SUB RELATIONSHIPS:
- Search government contracts database (USASpending.gov, SAM.gov) for subcontractor listings
- Identify: Who is prime contractor vs. subcontractor on major programs?
- Look for patterns: Does company X always sub to company Y?

2. JOINT VENTURES & STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS:
- Search for: joint press releases, MOU announcements, co-branded products
- Check LinkedIn: Cross-company teams, shared program managers, joint conference presentations
- Keywords: "partnership", "teaming agreement", "collaboration", "joint development", "LVC", "live virtual constructive", "synthetic training"

3. PLATFORM INTEGRATORS:
- Which aircraft OEMs (Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Northrop Grumman) are they partnered with?
- Search for: platform-specific HMD, AR, simulation, or training system integration programs (F-35, F-16, Apache, JLTV, training ranges)
- Are these exclusive relationships or competitive selections?

4. TECHNOLOGY LICENSING:
- Search patent databases for licensing agreements
- Look for: cross-references in patent applications, acknowledgment of licensed technology in technical papers

5. COMPETITIVE DYNAMICS:
- Where are these companies competing head-to-head vs. complementary?
- Check proposal win/loss records where publicly available (FOIA requests, trade publications)

6. FUTURE TEAMING INDICATORS:
- LinkedIn connections between BD teams across companies
- Joint conference booth appearances
- Shared membership in industry working groups (NDIA, AIA)

DEFENSE-SPECIFIC SOURCES:
- Check DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center) for technical reports
- Search Congressional Research Service reports for program background
- Review DoD budget justification books (PB-44s) for program funding
- Check DOTE annual reports for operational test results

SOURCE HIERARCHY (prioritize in this order):
TIER 1 (Highest reliability):
- Government contract databases
- Official company press releases
- Patent office records
- Peer-reviewed publications

TIER 2 (Good reliability):
- Trade publications (Defense News, Breaking Defense, Janes)
- Conference proceedings
- Company annual reports / 10-Ks

TIER 3 (Use with caution - mark as [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]):
- News aggregators
- Blog posts
- LinkedIn posts
- Forum discussions

VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
- All partnership claims must be supported by official company announcement, contract document, or multiple credible third-party sources
- Cross-reference all claims with at least 2 independent sources
- Flag any information found in only a single source as [SINGLE SOURCE - VERIFY]

HALLUCINATION PREVENTION:
- Do not infer or extrapolate beyond what sources explicitly state
- If you cannot find information, state "No data found" rather than estimating
- Mark any uncertainty with [CONFIDENCE: LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH]
- Provide exact quotes for critical claims, with source and date
- Do not assume partnerships exist based on circumstantial evidence

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Well-organized markdown file with:
- Clear sections for each investigation area
- Bullet points for each finding with inline source citations
- Network diagram showing relationships with strength indicators:
  - CONTRACTUAL/FORMAL: Documented teaming agreements, joint ventures, contracts
  - INFORMAL/EMERGING: Conference co-appearances, LinkedIn signals, industry group membership
- Timeline of relationship formation and any changes (partnerships ended, new teaming formed)
- Confidence Assessment rating each major finding as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW
